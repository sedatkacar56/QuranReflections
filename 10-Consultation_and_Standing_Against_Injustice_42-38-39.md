# Qur’an 42:38–39  
## Consultation and Standing Against Injustice

---

## 📖 Arabic (Relevant Portion of the Verses)

> **وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱسْتَجَابُوا۟ لِرَبِّهِمْ وَأَقَامُوا۟ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ  
> وَأَمْرُهُمْ شُورَىٰ بَيْنَهُمْ  
> وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَـٰهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ ۝  
> وَٱلَّذِينَ إِذَآ أَصَابَهُمُ ٱلْبَغْىُ هُمْ يَنتَصِرُونَ**

*(Shūrā 42:38–39)*

---

## 🌐 English (Meaning-Based Translation)

> **“…those who respond to their Lord, establish prayer,  
> conduct their affairs by mutual consultation,  
> and spend from what We have provided them;  
> and those who, when injustice befalls them, stand up for their rights.”**  
> *(Qur’an 42:38–39)*

---

## 🧠 Clear and Precise Explanation

In these verses, the Qur’an is **describing the character of believers**.  
It is **not giving slogans**, and it is **not encouraging passivity**.

---

### 1️⃣ “They conduct their affairs by mutual consultation”

This means that believers:

- do **not** rule by ego, impulse, or authoritarianism  
- do **not** impose decisions unilaterally  
- value **collective reasoning**, listening, and shared responsibility  

Consultation (**shūrā**) is presented as a **moral discipline**, not merely a social habit.  
It protects communities from **arrogance and tyranny**.

---

### 2️⃣ “When they are wronged, they stand up for their rights”

This does **not** mean aggression or revenge.

The Arabic verb **يَنتَصِرُونَ (yantaṣirūn)** means:

- seeking redress  
- defending oneself  
- standing up against injustice  

The Qur’an makes a clear point:

- Believers do **not normalize oppression**  
- They are **not required to remain silent**  
- They are **not praised for accepting injustice**  

At the same time, their response must remain **within the limits of justice**, not excess.

---

### 3️⃣ Why these two qualities appear together

This pairing is intentional:

- **Consultation** prevents oppression *from within* the community  
- **Standing up for one’s rights** prevents oppression *from outside*  

Together, they define a **balanced moral stance**:

- neither submissive  
- nor tyrannical  

---

## 🔑 Core Takeaway

**The Qur’an portrays believers as people who govern their lives through consultation and who refuse to accept injustice — while never becoming unjust themselves.**
