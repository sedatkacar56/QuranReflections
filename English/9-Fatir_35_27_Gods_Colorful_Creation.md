\# Fatir\_35\_27\_Gods\_Colorful\_Creation



---



\## 📖 ARABIC (Original)



> \*\*أَلَمْ تَرَ أَنَّ اللَّهَ أَنْزَلَ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ مَاءً فَأَخْرَجْنَا بِهِ ثَمَرَاتٍ مُخْتَلِفًا أَلْوَانُهَا ۖ  

> وَمِنَ الْجِبَالِ جُدَدٌ بِيضٌ وَحُمْرٌ وَغَرَابِيبُ سُودٌ مُّخْتَلِفٌ أَلْوَانُهَا\*\*  

> \*(Fâtır 35:27)\*



---



\## 🌐 ENGLISH (Direct Translation)



> \*\*"Do you not see that Allah sends down water from the sky and We bring forth with it fruits of varying colors? And in the mountains are streaks, white and red of varying shades, and black."\*\*  

> \*(Qur'an 35:27)\*



---



\## 🔍 Three Divine Signs (Verse Order)



\### 1️⃣ “Do you not see that Allah sends down water...”

\- ✅ Direct command: \*\*LOOK! OBSERVE! REFLECT!\*\*

\- ✅ Allah invites empirical observation  

\- ✅ Rainwater = Allah’s deliberate provision  

\- ✅ Nature reveals purposeful intelligent design  



---



\### 2️⃣ “…fruits of varying colors”

\- ✅ \*\*Single type of rainwater → endless fruit diversity\*\*

\- 🟡 Lemon 🟠 Orange 🔴 Apple  

\- 🟢 Grape ⚫ Olive 🟣 Plum  

\- 🟤 Fig 🟦 Blueberry  



\- ✅ Same water + same soil → different taste, smell, shape, benefit  

\- ✅ Infinite creativity from a uniform source = Divine mastery  



---



\### 3️⃣ “…mountains are streaks, white and red… black”

\- ✅ \*\*Geological stratification described precisely\*\*

\- ⚪ \*\*White streaks\*\* → limestone, quartz layers  

\- 🔴 \*\*Red streaks\*\* → iron oxide (hematite) formations  

\- ⚫ \*\*Black streaks\*\* → basalt, magnesium-rich stone  



\- ✅ Describes mountain cross-sections \*\*1400 years before geology\*\*



---
