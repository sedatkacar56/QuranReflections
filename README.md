# Qur’anic Reflections

This repository is a personal collection of reflections on selected Qur’anic verses.

I read verses from the Qur’an, pause, think, and write down what I understand from them.  
Sometimes I use AI as a tool to help me organize thoughts, clarify language, or structure ideas — but the responsibility for meaning, intention, and accuracy remains mine.

These notes are **not tafsīr** in the scholarly sense.  
They are reflections, observations, and lessons as I understand them at a given moment.

---

## Purpose

- To slow down while reading the Qur’an  
- To think carefully about meaning, context, and attitude  
- To write reflections primarily **for myself**
- To keep a personal archive that is independent of social media

If anyone else benefits from these reflections, they are welcome to read them.

---

## A note on humility and correction

These writings are **fallible**.

If you notice:
- a misunderstanding,
- an unclear expression,
- or something that conflicts with the Qur’an’s broader message,

please take it as a limitation of my understanding, not the text itself.

I remain open to correction and refinement.

---

## What this repository is NOT

- It is not a replacement for classical tafsīr
- It is not authoritative religious guidance
- It is not meant to argue, debate, or provoke

It is simply a space for careful thought and reflection.

---

## Structure

Reflections are written in Markdown and organized by:
- Surah
- Verse number

Each file usually contains:
- The verse (in English)
- Contextual explanation
- Reflections focused on attitude, meaning, and application

---

## Intention

The Qur’an repeatedly reminds us that guidance is not blocked by questions,  
but by arrogance, haste, and resistance to truth.

This repository is an attempt — however imperfect —  
to read, reflect, and write **with care**.
