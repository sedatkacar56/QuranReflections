# Bakara_286_Prayer_of_No_Burden

---

## 📖 ARABIC (Original)

> **رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَآ أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا ۚ  
> رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِنْ قَبْلِنَا ۚ  
> رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِ ۖ  
> وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا ۚ  
> أَنْتَ مَوْلَانَا فَانْصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ**

---

## 🌐 ENGLISH (Clear Translation)

> **"Our Lord, do not take us to account if we forget or make a mistake.  
> Our Lord, do not lay upon us a burden like that which You laid upon those before us.  
> Our Lord, do not burden us with what we have no ability to bear.  
> Pardon us, forgive us, and have mercy upon us.  
> You are our Protector, so give us victory over the disbelieving people."**  
> *(Qur'an 2:286)*

---

## 🇹🇷 TÜRKÇE (Modern / Anlamlı)

> **"Rabbimiz! Unutur veya hata edersek bizi sorumlu tutma.  
> Rabbimiz! Bizden öncekilere yüklediğin gibi bize ağır yükler yükleme.  
> Rabbimiz! Gücümüzün yetmeyeceği şeyleri bize taşıtma.  
> Bizi affet, bizi bağışla ve bize rahmet et.  
> Sen bizim Mevlâmızsın; inkârcı topluluğa karşı bize yardım et."**  
> *(Bakara 2:286)*

---

## 🔍 5 Clear Requests (Beautiful Structure)

| **ARABIC** | **ENGLISH** | **TÜRKÇE** |
|------------|-------------|------------|
| **لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا** | Don't hold us accountable | **Bizi sorumlu tutma** |
| **لَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا** | Don't burden like before | **Ağır yükler yükleme** |
| **لَا تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ** | Don't give beyond capacity | **Yetmeyecek yük verme** |
| **وَاعْفُ وَاغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْنَا** | Pardon, forgive, have mercy | **Affet, bağışla, merhamet et** |
| **أَنْتَ مَوْلَانَا فَانْصُرْنَا** | You are our Protector, help us | **Mevlâmızsın, yardım et** |

---

## 💎 Why This Duʿāʾ Is Perfect

- **HONEST** → Admits human weakness (forgetting, error)  
- **HUMBLE** → References those who failed before  
- **PRACTICAL** → Acknowledges human limits  
- **COMPREHENSIVE** → Covers pardon, forgiveness, mercy, and help  
- **TRUSTING** → Ends with *“You are our Protector”*

## 🕊️ Prophet’s Addition (Hadith)

**Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught to add:**

> **"Allahumme inni aʿūdhu bika min al-hammi wa al-ḥazan…"**  
> *(O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief…)*

**Making it the longest and most comprehensive duʿāʾ.**
