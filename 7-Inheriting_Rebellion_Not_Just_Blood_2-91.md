# Inheriting_Rebellion_Not_Just_Blood

---

> **"When it is said to them: 'Believe in what God has revealed,' they reply,  
> 'We only believe in what was sent down to us,' and they deny what came afterwards,  
> though it is truth confirming their own Scriptures!  
> Ask them, O Prophet,  
> 'Why then did you kill God's prophets before, if you are truly believers?'"**  
> *(Qur'an 2:91)*

---

## 📖 The Provocative Question

**Some Jews** rejected the Qur'an saying:  
*“We only accept what came to us (Torah). Nothing new.”*

**Allah’s shocking response:**  
*“Then why did YOU kill the prophets — if you’re true believers?”*

**🔥 Key fact:**  
These people **did not physically murder** any prophets.  
That happened **generations earlier**.

**So why hold them accountable?**

---

## 🧠 Qur'anic Accountability Logic

**Allah is not talking about physical hands holding knives.**  
**He is talking about moral alignment with crime.**

- Physical murder → generations ago  
- Moral rebellion → happening **now**

**You inherit blame when you:**

- ✅ **Approve** your ancestors’ rejection of truth  
- ✅ **Defend** their hostility toward prophets  
- ✅ **Repeat** the same rejection toward new guidance  
- ✅ **Reject** the same kind of divine message  

---

## 🪞 Their Claim vs. Their History

**They said:**  
*“We only believe in what came to us”* ✅

**Allah replied:**  
*“Then why was your history filled with prophet-murder?”* ❌

- Claim → *“We’re faithful to revelation”*  
- Reality → *“We killed those who brought revelation”*

**Contradiction = rebellion, not faithfulness.**

---

## ⚖️ Attitude Inheritance = Moral Responsibility

**The Qur'an is crystal clear:**

- Bloodline alone ≠ guilt  
- Rebellious stance = guilt  

**Examples of inheriting blame:**

- **Defending** prophet-killing as “necessary”  
- **Justifying** rejection of Muhammad ﷺ just as earlier prophets were rejected  
- **Repeating** the attitude: *“We only accept OUR revelation”*  

**Anyone who rejects this legacy is clean.**

---

## 🚫 What the Qur'an Does NOT Teach

- ❌ *“All Jews are guilty forever”* → Blood guilt  
- ❌ *“Children pay for parents’ sins”* → Genetic punishment  
- ✅ *“Same rebellion today = same accountability”* → Moral continuity  

---

## 💡 The Simple Test

- Do you **REPEAT** their mistake? → You own it  
- Do you **REPENT** from their error? → You’re free  

**Same attitude = same destination.**

---

## 🌐 Why This Matters Today

**Modern applications:**

- *“Our denomination only”* → Same tribal rejection  
- *“Our scholars are final”* → Same prophet-resistance  
- *“Our way or nothing”* → Same revelation-rejection  

**Allah judges ATTITUDE, not ancestry.**

---

## 🕊️ The Final Balance

**Qur'an 2:91 teaches:**

- Rebellion is a **choice**, not a bloodline  
- Truth-rejection is **your stance**, not your father’s  
- Every generation faces the same test:  
  *“Will you accept Allah’s messenger?”*

**Your answer determines your accountability.**
